## What we are building
ECHO: ONE OF ONE — a playable, ~10-minute third-person cyberpunk adventure, built as a Qollab-native HTML/JS/Three.js project (`echo/` in the repo) with real Python/Qiskit physics running locally in the browser via Pyodide. Rin Vale must deliver an unknown quantum state that cannot be copied: she fails to clone it at a replication booth (no-cloning), learns at a relay that entanglement alone sends no message (no-signalling), then at a rooftop terminal uses a pre-shared Bell pair + Bell measurement + two classical bits + X/Z corrections to teleport it, consuming the original, and the city's lights return. The full spec is the owner's `ECHO_MASTER_VISION.md` (normative physics: §7; engineering: §8; quest states: §9; cut list: §13), with `ROADMAP_48_HOURS.md`, `QOLLAB_DEPLOYMENT.md`, `CLAUDE_CODE_PROMPTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` at the repo root. Read them; do not restate them.

**Who uses it:** Hackathon judges (Qollab × IonQ; rubric: novelty, technical execution, impact, documentation) and first-time players with no physics background, in a desktop browser on Qollab.
**The bar:** A blind tester finishes title → three missions → ending on Qollab without help and can answer the three teach-back questions; quantum tests pass; one honest selected-backend job; 3 consecutive full runs; ≥30 FPS on a mid-range laptop (vision §1.4, §12.5, §17).
